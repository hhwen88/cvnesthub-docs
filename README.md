- 🤖 **AI Resume Optimizer**: Upload your PDF, and our AI extracts, restructures, and rewrites bullet points using action verbs and metrics.
- 🎯 **ATS Compatibility Check**: Get a 5-dimension score to ensure your resume passes automated filters.
- 📝 **20+ Professional Templates**: Clean, ATS-friendly American & British style templates.
- ✉️ **AI Cover Letters**: Generate tailored cover letters in seconds based on your resume and job description.
- 🔄 **Job Match Analysis**: Paste a JD, get a match score and specific rewrite suggestions.
- 🌍 **Multi-Language Support**: English, Chinese, Español, Deutsch.
